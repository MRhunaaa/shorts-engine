What makes your sample feel alive

Light tells the mood

Blue moonlight at night against a warm torch cone on the floor, golden window shafts by day, lens flare and glowing props (headlamp, lamp).

Rooms full of story props

Wallpaper, framed pictures, plant, curtains, patterned rug, fluffy carpet, checker tiles, fridge magnets, a "DON'T MOVE!" note, wall phone, calendar, a cat.

Extreme, close cameras

Wide lens very close to faces, worm's-eye shots from the floor, top-down views, dutch tilts, and a camera that dives from a face down to a couch leg.

Big faces, soft shading

Heads fill a third of the frame, thick outlines, very expressive brows, clothes with gradient shading instead of flat fills.

Transitions with a lens

A glass-bubble distortion, blurred zoom-throughs and ghost dissolves between places.
