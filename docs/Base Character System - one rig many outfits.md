# Base character system: one rig, many outfits

User's 2nd sample ("why the second draw always wins", 56 s, 1080x1920): every character shares ONE base body
(big pale round head, bean torso, very long thin legs, black dot hands); identity comes only from clothes and
accessories (cowboy hats, moustaches, glasses, vests, holsters, belts, badges). Efficient (one rig) and on-brand.

## Our base (different from the sample)
- Warm egg head with a shine, rosy cheeks, button nose, ears.
- **Glossy bead eyes** (black ovals, 2 white glints) + eyebrows in the character's hair colour.
- **Varied skin tones** (international), not one pale colour.
- Wide **pear/bean torso** with gradient + sheen, short neck, **long thin legs**, **round skin-coloured hands**.
- Outfit layers: garment (inside torso clip), belt, hat, hair back/front, facial hair, prop.

## Outfits drawn (page: `vault/pages/cast-system.html`, images `out/system_a.png`, `out/system_b.png`)
BASE, Sheriff, Scientist, Chef, King, Astronaut, Pirate, Detective, Farmer, Office Guy.

## Why this helps virality / production
- Consistent channel identity: viewers recognise the "family" in every thumbnail.
- Any story's cast is just new outfits on the same rig: faster production, same lip-sync and poses.
- The silhouette hook comes from hats and props.
