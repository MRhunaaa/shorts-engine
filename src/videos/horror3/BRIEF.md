## The story (TRUE, documentary; do not invent facts on screen)
Sources: merdeka.com 13/7/2022 ("Kisah Pendaki Bertahan Hidup Setelah Hilang Empat Hari di Gunung Arjuno"), iNews.id 31/5/2025.
Muhammad Naam Kurniawan, 34, experienced hiker from Pandaan (Pasuruan). March 2022, group of 5 met online, via Tambaksari Purwosari. Summit Puncak Ogal-Agil (Gunung Arjuno, 3,339 mdpl). On the way down a friend twisted an ankle and fainted; Naam went ahead to get help. Around 14.00, above Plewangan (savanna), fog came suddenly; he says there was no wind, no sound, the leaves did not move. He got lost in the Lalijiwo area. At dusk he says he saw people in royal-style clothing passing, a crowd like a market, people sitting in a pendopo (iNews: a king sitting still in front of many people; huge trees with aerial roots; fog until dawn). He stayed silent and ate nothing: he believed food from "the other world" traps you there. On day 4 (Wednesday), at the bottom of a ravine, he heard people talking, shouted "Tolong Pak!", was answered and told to climb up. He was found by a villager checking his rice-field irrigation channel, at Alas Sriti, Dusun Sumbul, Desa Klampok, Singosari (Malang), weak, hypothermic; villagers carried him to the settlement.

## THE BAR (user's words: "detail kecil yang banyak, 20x lebih banyak, ukuran sempurna, gak kegedean, gak ngacak, gak aneh, harus bagus, lebih kerasa")
- **Density:** every shot gets 40+ distinct small details, many of them alive (slow, eased, deterministic): moss strands, lichen dots, dew drops that catch light, ferns, grass tussocks blade by blade, edelweiss, cantigi leaves, hanging moss beards, roots, pebbles, footprints, bootlace ends, pack straps + buckles + carabiner, zipper pulls, stitching, beanie ribs, breath vapour, insects (moth, beetle, spider + web), birds, bats, fireflies, falling leaves, water drips, ripples, smoke, fabric folds, batik motifs, frayed pita rafia trail markers, rust, cracks, stains. Layer them at 3+ parallax depths (0.6 / 1.0 / 1.6) with fog between layers so depth reads.
- **Sizes (the user always checks):** standing adult wide = 0.35 H (672 px), medium = 0.6 H, close-up head = 0.45 H. Head = 0.13 of body (the rig does this; NEVER scale a head separately). Same person = same size within a shot type. Props in real proportion to the figure (a backpack ≈ 0.33 of height, a tray ≈ 0.25, a lantern ≈ 0.1). Depth scaling must be consistent (farther = smaller AND higher on the ground plane). Nothing oversized, nothing floating unless intended (ghost feet), no random placement that overlaps the action.
- **Calm + smooth:** only eased motion (move/settle), no per-frame random, no fast moves, no flashes (the engine owns the only sting at the smash). Everyone breathes (idle). Wind via gust(t), and in fog/lost multiply every sway by breeze(t) (the "no wind" beat must visibly freeze everything).
- **Readable:** frame 0 of each shot must already show its subject. Keep zones clear: header + labels y 200-430 at left (x < 700), beat stamps y 460-620 centre (rescue/doubt only), captions y 1110-1460 centre (put calm background there, no key action), bottom 22 % and right 150 px between y 900-1600 = TikTok UI (nothing important).
- **Look:** the "TINTA SENTER" style of Horror 1/2: dark pass = moonlit navy, lit pass = cream paper woodcut ink, light map decides what is seen. Code-drawn only (no images). Palette `p.*` from horror1/paint PAL. Red only for the supernatural/beats.
- **Performance:** full-res render must stay ≤ ~2.5 s/frame on this 4-thread PC (5 sub-frames per frame!). Pre-render STATIC detail (rocks, moss textures, grass fields, tree bark, distant ridges, terraces) ONCE into module-level offscreen canvases cached per pass (`p.mode`) and per layer, then drawImage them each frame with parallax; draw only the moving details live. Never `ctx.filter = blur` over layers of thousands of strokes (it hung a render) — blur only small foreground pieces or pre-blur into the cache. Deterministic: use `hash()` seeds, never Math.random.
